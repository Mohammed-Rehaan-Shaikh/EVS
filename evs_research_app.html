The Tripartite Logic

A fundamental architectural shift in how water is perceived and managed.

Retain

Converting parks into retention basins to hold peak rainwater volume.

Delay

Using rain gardens and green roofs to slow flow and prevent system surges.

Drain

Guiding excess surface water safely to the harbor via cloudburst boulevards.
